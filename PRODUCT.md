# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step), same as the agency's other client sites.

## Users

- Municipalities, public institutions, farms, industries and households in Ñuble, Biobío, Maule and parts of La Araucanía that urgently need drinking water (shortage, contamination, wildfire) — often on a phone, under stress, wanting to call now.
- Companies and contractors hiring machinery, cargo transport or industrial/agricultural/forestry support (less urgent, comparing providers).

## Product Purpose

Website of Aljibe (aljibe.cl), San Carlos (KM 370 Ruta 5 Sur). Since 2013 distributes certified drinking water by tanker truck and provides cargo transport, machinery rental/operation and industrial/agricultural/forestry services. Success = a visitor in an emergency reaches a phone call or WhatsApp within seconds.

## Positioning

Regional operator with certified DS41 stainless tankers (1.000 to 40.000 L), 24/7 response, rapid response to wildfire emergencies, and Giro Limpio certified fleet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services:
1. Traslado y distribución de agua potable (DS41 MINSAL certification, expert operators, tanks 1.000–40.000 L).
2. Servicio de maquinaria: excavadora, camión tolva, retroexcavadora, motoniveladora, minicargador (rental, maintenance, operation).
3. Transporte de carga nacional: camiones planos, camión tolva, camionetas, maquinaria en general.
4. Servicios industriales, agrícolas y forestales: equipo con aspersor, personal calificado, vehículos de reemplazo, soporte 24 h, respuesta rápida ante incendios.

Mission/vision/values (Compromiso, Responsabilidad, Calidad, Eficiencia, Sostenibilidad) from current site.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Aljibe", tagline "Distribución de agua potable". Existing logo is a blue water drop (img/logo.png from aljibe.cl).
- Client asked for a much more dynamic site (moving elements, services that slide), with transportesnazar.cl as reference, including a staff ("Nuestra gente") section in the same style.

1. Contact first: call/WhatsApp visible from every scroll position and on every device.
2. Emergency clarity over decoration: motion must never delay or hide the contact path.
3. Only state facts the client confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
